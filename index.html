About COLM

An academic venue for the study of language modeling, broadly defined.

The leap in general-purpose capabilities from language models is a landmark in the development of AI, and it raises a different set of research questions than related venues typically focus on. COLM is an academic venue focused on the study of language modeling, broadly defined — building a community of researchers across disciplines who understand, improve, and critique the development of LM technology.

Venue & format

  • Hilton Union Square, San Francisco, USA
  • Main conference: October 6–8, 2026 (three single-track days of invited talks, orals, and posters)
  • Workshops: October 9, 2026 (about 18 workshops, 7–9 hours each)
  • In-person only — there is no virtual attendance option

How do you pronounce COLM? It is pronounced like "Collum", after the Irish name Colm, which means dove.

FAQ

The most common questions about submitting to and attending COLM.

Can I submit to COLM while my paper is under review elsewhere?expand_more
No — COLM does not allow double submissions. A full paper under review at COLM cannot be simultaneously under review elsewhere (e.g. ICML/NeurIPS), and any ARR paper at any stage counts as under submission. Double-submission checks run after the full-paper deadline. Concurrent submission to non-archival workshops is fine.
Will there be a short / position / survey paper track?expand_more
There is no separate short-paper track and no separate call or reviewing for position papers in 2026, opting for simplicity. There is no special survey track either, but you may submit a survey and it will be routed to appropriate area chairs and reviewers.
Can I edit my submission between the abstract and paper deadlines?expand_more
Yes — all fields stay editable until the full-paper deadline, but keep edits minimal to avoid disrupting the program committee's work.
What is the preprint policy?expand_more
Non-anonymous preprints (arXiv, social media, websites) are allowed. Reviewers are told not to actively search for them, and encountering one is not a conflict of interest. You may submit work that is already a preprint without citing it, but related prior published work by the authors must be cited with appropriate anonymization.
What is the reciprocal reviewing requirement?expand_more
Two requirements. Per-submission: each submission is expected to contribute a reviewer (the form lets you designate one or mark an exemption). Per-reviewer: any author on 4+ submissions is automatically added to the reviewer pool unless already on the program committee or organization. The aim is to scale the reviewer pool with submission volume.
What qualifications are required to review?expand_more
Reviewers should have research experience equivalent to a second-year ML grad student and have been a primary author on at least two peer-reviewed papers at related venues (e.g. ACL, EMNLP, ICML, NeurIPS, ICLR). First-time reviewers are strongly encouraged to find a qualified, experienced mentor.
What is the expected reviewing load?expand_more
Up to about 5 papers per reviewer.
Posters or oral presentations? And poster size?expand_more
All papers are presented as posters; a small number are selected for orals (authors notified around mid-September). Posters are 36 in (H) × 72 in (W).
Can I present remotely or have someone hang my poster?expand_more
No remote presentation, and the conference cannot place your poster for you. COLM is in-person only and submission entails a responsibility to present. If you truly cannot attend, the recommended option is to find a colleague who is attending to present on your behalf.
Is there an author registration requirement?expand_more
At least one author of each accepted paper must register for the main conference (workshop-only registration does not count). Students need only a student registration, and one registration covers an author even with multiple accepted papers.
Do postdocs / recent grads qualify for the student rate?expand_more
If you are a recent graduate publishing under your student affiliation, register as a Student. Other non-industry researchers — including postdocs, faculty, and independent researchers — should register as Academic.
Is there a virtual option? How do I get a visa letter?expand_more
COLM is in-person only, with no virtual attendance option. Upon registration you can download an invitation letter for visa purposes.
Is COLM archival?expand_more
Yes, COLM is an archival venue. You may also submit to non-archival venues, but not to other archival venues.
What is COLM's policy on the use of LLMs?expand_more
COLM 2026 adopts ICLR 2026's LLM policy (modified so minor writing/coding assistance need not be disclosed). Substantive use — originating ideas, writing original content or references, generating data/plots, or evaluation — must be disclosed. In reviewing, ANY LLM use must be disclosed. Authors and reviewers remain responsible for their contributions.
How do you pronounce COLM?expand_more
Like "Collum", after the Irish name Colm — which means dove.
